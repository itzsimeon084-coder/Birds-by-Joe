About Us

Passionate about birds. Dedicated to their care.

Birds by Joe

Welcome to Birds by Joe, where our passion for beautiful companion birds is at the heart of everything we do.

We specialize in hand raised baby parrots and provide a selection of birds, supplies and information for bird lovers.

Our Story

Birds have always been more than just pets. They are intelligent, social and remarkable companions.

Our goal is to provide customers with healthy, well cared for birds while helping new bird owners understand the commitment and care involved in keeping a companion bird.

From selecting the right species to providing appropriate food, cages and accessories, we strive to make the experience as enjoyable and informative as possible.

Experience & Dedication

Our approach is based on careful attention to the birds in our care and a commitment to helping customers find the right companion for their lifestyle.

Hand Raised Baby Parrots
