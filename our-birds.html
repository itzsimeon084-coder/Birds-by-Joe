Bird Species

Birds by Joe specializes in hand raised baby parrots and other companion birds.

We specialize in African Greys, Amazons, Caiques, Conures, Pionus, Cape Parrots, Cockatoos and Macaws.

We also raise smaller birds such as Finches, Canaries, Parrotlets and Cockatiels.
