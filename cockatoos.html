Cockatoos

Cockatoos are intelligent, social and highly interactive parrots. The original Birds by Joe page features several Cockatoo varieties, including Bare-Eyed, Sulphur Crested, Umbrella and Goffin Cockatoos.

Bare-Eyed Cockatoos

Size

Most Bare-Eyed Cockatoos are approximately 14 to 16 inches in length, measured from the tip of the tail feathers to the beak.

Average Lifespan

The average Bare-Eyed Cockatoo lives for around 50 years, although they can live much longer under appropriate conditions.

Temperament

Bare-Eyed Cockatoos have a goofy and clownish nature. They are especially known for playing, performing tricks and socializing.

They generally adjust well to their surroundings and are friendly with their owners. They are also intelligent and known for their talking abilities.

Colors

Their main color is white, with pink or salmon-like patches near their beaks. They also have gray patches around their eyes and horn-colored beaks.

Feeding

Bare-Eyed Cockatoos can be prone to obesity and weight problems, so a suitable low-fat diet is important. Leafy greens, root vegetables, fresh fruit, nuts and pellets can form part of their diet, together with plenty of clean drinking water.

Exercise

These birds require plenty of exercise. Around three hours of playtime outside the cage is recommended on the original page. They enjoy moving around with toys and interacting with their owners.

Bare-Eyed Cockatoos as Pets

Although their appearance may seem less colorful than some other parrots, their playful, sweet and intelligent personality makes them popular companion birds.

Sulphur Crested Cockatoos

There are two prominent subspecies of Sulphur Crested Cockatoos: Lesser and Greater Sulphur Crested Cockatoos.

Greater Sulphur Crested Cockatoos can reach almost 20 inches in length, while Lesser Sulphur Crested Cockatoos are generally around 13 to 15 inches.

Lifespan

Sulphur Crested Cockatoos can live for around 80 years and may live even longer under the right conditions.

Colors

These Cockatoos are predominantly white with black beaks and beautiful yellow crest feathers.

Feeding and Exercise

In captivity, they benefit from nutritious vegetables, fruits, seeds and quality pellets. Their diet needs to be carefully managed to avoid nutritional problems.

They are active birds and require several hours of exercise and play every day. Open space, stands and perches can help them move, stretch and exercise.

Sulphur Crested Cockatoos as Pets

Their intelligent, active and playful nature makes them highly social companion birds. They require significant attention and interaction from their owners.

Umbrella Cockatoos

Size

Umbrella Cockatoos are large parrots and normally reach around 24 inches in length.

Average Lifespan

With appropriate care and attention, these Cockatoos can live for around 80 years in captivity.

Temperament

Umbrella Cockatoos have a gentle and sweet temperament. They can be affectionate and enjoy spending time with their owners.

Colors

They are predominantly white, with yellow shades beneath their wings. They have black or grayish feet and beaks and light bluish eye rings.

Feeding

Fresh vegetables, fruits, grains, sprouts, nuts, seeds and quality food are part of the nutritional requirements described on the original page. Fresh water should always be available.

Exercise

Umbrella Cockatoos need plenty of play and exercise time. Strong chewable toys can help provide appropriate beak activity.

Flying, structured games and free movement can help them exercise their muscles. They also enjoy affection and interaction with their owners.

Umbrella Cockatoos as Pets

Umbrella Cockatoos are calm, social, charming and intelligent birds when properly trained and given sufficient affection and attention.

Goffin Cockatoos

Size

Goffin Cockatoos are among the smallest Cockatoo species, reaching approximately 12 to 13 inches from the tail feathers to the beak.

Average Lifespan

The Goffin Cockatoo can live for an average of around 30 years.

Temperament

Goffin Cockatoos are calm, social, affectionate, playful and inquisitive when given appropriate training and guidance.

They require regular interaction and attention from their owners, particularly because of their active nature.

Colors

Goffin Cockatoos are predominantly white with pink or salmon-like coloring around the beak.

Feeding

They benefit from a carefully managed, nutritious and relatively low-fat diet. Vegetables, leafy greens, roots, fruits, quality pellets, seeds and grains are among the foods discussed on the original page.

Proper nutrition is particularly important because this species can experience weight gain problems.
