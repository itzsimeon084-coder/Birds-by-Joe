Conures

Conures are colorful, active and social companion birds. Below are the Conure varieties featured on the Birds by Joe page.

Sun Conure

If you are in the market and looking for stunningly beautiful birds, Sun Conures are among the best options. They have a blend of rich and bold colors all over the body.

From yellow to orange, these birds feature bright colors throughout their body, with dark red-orange highlights around the belly and cheeks.

Their wings feature green splashes over yellow, while the wingtips are bluish. The tail contains both blue and green colors.

Sun Conures measure around 12 inches on average and weigh approximately 100-130 grams. Their average lifespan is around 25-30 years.

They are highly sociable and affectionate birds. They require medium to large bird cages because they are active and social.

Sun Conures enjoy entertaining their owners with acrobatic tricks and activities. One remarkable quality is their distinctive screeching vocalizations.

Jenday Conure

Jenday Conures are similar to Sun Conures in both appearance and personality.

Their heads are solid yellowish while their wings and upper body have bright green coloration.

These birds have a red-orange belly while the tail has a bluish-green color.

Jenday Conures measure around 12 inches and weigh approximately 100-130 grams. Their average lifespan is around 25-30 years.

They require a similar cage size to Sun Conures and are highly social and active.

Jenday Conures enjoy performing tricks and spending time outside their cages with their owners.

Nanday Conure

Nanday Conures do not have the same vibrant colors as Sun and Jenday Conures, but their personality makes up for it.

Their head is black while the wings and body are primarily green. Blue colors can be seen around the tail and flight feathers.

Bright red coloration may also appear around the legs.

Nanday Conures typically weigh around 135-145 grams and have an average lifespan of approximately 25-30 years.

They are approximately 13 inches long and require an appropriately sized cage with sufficient height.

They are social and active birds. Smaller, cramped spaces can cause distress and may result in noisy or destructive behavior.

Green Cheek Conure

The Green Cheek Conure has green coloration across much of its body, together with grey breasts and maroon tails.

Their primary wing feathers are bluish and their cheeks are green. The head is darker than the rest of the body.

Mutations are common when Green Cheek Conures are raised in captivity. Color variations can include yellow, turquoise and cinnamon.

They are approximately 10 inches long and weigh around 65-85 grams.

Their average lifespan is around 15 years, although they can live longer with proper care.

Rather than producing frequent screeches, these birds are more accustomed to producing sharp whistles.

Green Cheek Conures have a quiet and friendly nature while still enjoying attention, making them suitable for apartments and homes.

Peach Fronted Conure

Peach Fronted Conures have green coloration across their upper body together with greyish-green breasts.

Their belly has a light green coloration. Their green wings have black tips while the tail is blue and green.

The head is completely green except for the crown, which is bright orange with blue sides around the eye area.

Their black beak helps distinguish them from Half-Moon and Orange-Fronted Conures.

Peach Fronted Conures are approximately 10 inches long and weigh around 105 grams. Their average lifespan is approximately 25-30 years.

These birds love to play and enjoy outdoor activity. They can make lovely pets for experienced bird owners.

They are also relatively quiet and do not produce the same screeching sounds as some other Conures, making them suitable for apartment environments.

Maroon Bellied Conure

Maroon Bellied Conures are small birds with a green-colored body.

Their breasts feature a greenish-yellow band that can also be seen along their sides.

The primary flight feathers are green-blue. The tail is light maroon underneath and green on top.

Brownish patches can be seen around their ears.

These birds weigh around 65-85 grams and generally measure around 10 inches from the head to the tail.

Their average lifespan is generally around 20 years.

Because of their size, they require a medium-to-small cage with appropriate bar spacing.

Maroon Bellied Conures are social, intelligent, inquisitive and affectionate birds. They are also relatively quiet compared with some other Conure species.
