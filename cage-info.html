Cage Information

Choosing the right home for your feathered companion

Choosing The Right Cage

Choosing an appropriate cage is one of the most important decisions you can make for your bird. The cage should provide enough room for movement, exercise, feeding and comfortable resting.

Different species have different requirements, so always consider the bird's size, activity level, beak strength and spacing requirements before purchasing a cage.

General Cage Size Guide

Bird Type Suggested Cage Bar Spacing Important Consideration
Budgies / Small Finches Spacious flight cage Narrow spacing Prevent escape or injury
Cockatiels Medium flight cage Species appropriate spacing Allow room for flying
Conures / Caiques Medium to large cage Appropriate for beak size Strong construction
African Grey / Amazon Large cage Appropriate for species Strong bars and locks
Macaws / Large Cockatoos Extra-large cage Heavy-duty spacing Extremely strong construction

Cage Requirements By Bird

Budgies

Budgies are active birds and benefit from a cage that provides plenty of horizontal space for movement and flying.

Cockatiels

Choose a spacious cage with enough room for the bird's long tail and enough space between perches.

Conures

Conures require a strong cage with adequate room for climbing, playing and exercising.

African Greys

African Greys need a durable, spacious cage with strong bars and secure doors.

Amazons

Provide plenty of room for movement, climbing and toys while ensuring the cage is strong enough for the bird's powerful beak.

Macaws

Large parrots require very spacious and heavy-duty cages designed for their size and strength.

Important Cage Tips

Size Matters

Choose the largest practical cage that fits your available space and your bird's needs.

Bar Spacing

Bar spacing must be appropriate for the species. Incorrect spacing can create a serious safety risk.

Strong Construction

Larger parrots need cages made from durable materials that can withstand their powerful beaks.

Secure Doors

Make sure doors and feeding openings are secure and cannot easily be opened by an intelligent bird.

Perches

Provide appropriately sized perches and vary their position to encourage movement.

Toys & Enrichment

Birds need appropriate toys and enrichment to stay active and mentally stimulated.

A Comfortable Environment

A cage should be more than simply a place where your bird is kept. It should provide a safe and comfortable environment where the bird can eat, sleep, play and interact with its surroundings.

Position the cage in a suitable area away from dangerous drafts, excessive heat and household hazards.

Always follow species-specific care recommendations when setting up your bird's environment.
