Cockatiels

Cockatiels are social and entertaining companion birds known for their friendly personalities and excellent whistling ability.

They can make affectionate household pets and are particularly well known for their interaction with their owners.

Budgies

Budgies, also known as budgerigars, are small, colorful and social birds that have become extremely popular household pets.

They are active, friendly and can develop impressive talking and whistling abilities.
