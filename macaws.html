Macaws

Macaws are intelligent, colorful and highly social parrots. Below are the Macaw varieties featured on the original Birds by Joe page.

Blue & Gold Macaws

You may also know the Blue Gold Macaw as the Blue and Yellow Macaw or the Blue Macaw. Their striking combination of blue and yellow makes them one of the most recognizable Macaws.

Hand-fed babies are especially appealing to bird owners because of their intelligence, playful personalities and social nature. Blue and Gold Macaws require proper nutrition, plenty of space and regular interaction.

Because of their large size, a spacious cage and plenty of toys are important for their wellbeing.

Catalina Macaws

The Catalina Macaw is a hybrid between the Blue and Gold Macaw and the Scarlet Macaw. Their appearance can vary depending on the parent birds.

Catalina Macaws can inherit personality characteristics from both parent breeds. They are generally playful, social and eager to learn.

Due to their size and active nature, they require a large, secure cage with appropriate toys and enrichment.

Greenwing Macaws

Greenwing Macaws are known for their beautiful combination of red, green and blue feathers. Their appearance is sometimes confused with Scarlet Macaws.

Greenwing Macaws have green wing feathers and distinctive red feather lines across their facial area.

These intelligent and gentle birds still have a playful and mischievous side and benefit from large cages and plenty of chewable toys.

Hahn's Macaws

Hahn's Macaw is a small member of the Red-Shouldered Macaw group and is often referred to as a mini Macaw.

They are considerably smaller than the large Macaw species, making them attractive to bird lovers looking for a smaller companion.

Hahn's Macaws are social and friendly and can be suitable for experienced bird owners who understand their care requirements.

Harlequin Macaws

Harlequin Macaws are hybrids resulting from breeding Blue and Gold Macaws with Greenwing Macaws.

They can inherit characteristics from both parent breeds and are known for their colorful appearance.

Their playful, affectionate and social personalities make them appealing companion birds.

The Hyacinth Macaw

The Hyacinth Macaw is one of the most impressive and sought-after Macaw species. It is famous for its vivid bluish-violet coloration and enormous size.

Despite their powerful beaks and impressive physical strength, Hyacinth Macaws are often described as gentle and affectionate birds.

They require substantial space, specialized care and an owner prepared for the needs of a very large parrot.

Military Macaws

Military Macaws have predominantly green plumage with distinctive facial and wing markings.

Their strong beaks, intelligence and social nature make them interesting companion birds.

Military Macaws benefit from interaction, enrichment and appropriate toys to keep them mentally and physically occupied.

Red-Fronted Macaws

Red-Fronted Macaws are medium-sized Macaws with striking red, orange and blue coloring.

Their colorful appearance becomes increasingly distinctive as they mature.

They are intelligent, active birds that can provide plenty of entertainment when given sufficient space and appropriate enrichment.

Scarlet Macaws

Scarlet Macaws are famous for their brilliant red coloration combined with yellow and blue wing feathers.

Their appearance makes them one of the most recognizable Macaws.

Scarlet Macaws can have strong personalities and require knowledgeable handling, proper socialization and consistent care.

Severe Macaws

The Severe Macaw belongs to the mini-Macaw category and is one of the larger members of that group.

Their plumage is primarily green with additional blue and reddish coloring.

They are active, social and intelligent birds that enjoy toys and interaction. Proper stimulation is important to help prevent boredom and destructive behavior.

Yellow-Collared Macaws

Yellow-Collared Macaws are smaller Macaws with large personalities. They are predominantly green and develop a distinctive yellow collar around the neck.

They are active and social birds and can learn words and phrases with appropriate interaction and training.

Their smaller size makes them an interesting option for bird lovers who want a Macaw without the size of the largest species.
