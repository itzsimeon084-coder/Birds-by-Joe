Finches

Finches are small, colorful and social birds that make excellent companions for people who prefer smaller pets.

They are relatively low-maintenance and are known for their playful and active nature.

There are many different varieties and color mutations of finches, giving bird enthusiasts plenty of choices.

Native/Natural Habitat

Finches can be found across many parts of the world and different species have adapted to very different environments.

Their natural habitats range from dry regions and grasslands to forests and tropical environments.

Different varieties can be found across North America, South America, Europe, Africa and Asia.

Personality & Behavior

Finches are social birds and generally enjoy the company of other finches.

They are best suited to environments where they have enough room to fly and move around.

Unlike many parrots, finches generally do not enjoy frequent handling by humans.

Keeping compatible finches together can help provide companionship and reduce stress.

They are active birds that enjoy flying, exploring and interacting with other birds.

Speech & Sounds

Finches are vocal birds, but their sounds are considerably softer than those of many larger parrots.

Their cheerful chirping and singing can be entertaining without becoming excessively loud.

Their vocalizations are one reason they can work well for people living in apartments or other shared environments.

Gouldian Finch

Gouldian Finches are famous for their spectacular combination of bright colors.

They are also known as Rainbow Finches or Lady Gouldian Finches.

They are social, intelligent and active birds, although they generally prefer the company of other birds rather than extensive handling.

Both males and females display beautiful colors, although males are usually more intensely colored.

Their plumage can include combinations of red, black, yellow, green, purple and blue.

Gouldian Finches are small birds, generally measuring approximately four to six inches.

They typically weigh only a few grams and can live for many years when provided with good nutrition and appropriate care.

Zebra Finches

Zebra Finches are among the most popular varieties of finches kept as household pets.

They are active, playful and attractive small birds.

Zebra Finches are native to Australia and are especially well adapted to dry environments.

Their sexes can normally be distinguished by their different markings.

Males commonly display orange cheek patches, chest markings and distinctive barring, while females have a simpler appearance.

Both sexes have attractive orange-red beaks.

They are small birds, normally measuring around ten centimeters and weighing roughly twelve grams.

Their cheerful chatter makes them enjoyable birds to keep.

European Goldfinches

European Goldfinches are colorful passerine birds belonging to the finch family.

They occur naturally across parts of Europe, North Africa and Central Asia.

Their striking appearance makes them easy to identify.

They have a red face, black and white head markings and dark wings with yellow sections.

They are social and intelligent birds that can enjoy the company of compatible birds.

Adult European Goldfinches are generally around twelve to fourteen centimeters long.

Their average weight is approximately fourteen to nineteen grams.

With proper nutrition and care, they can live for many years.

Society Finches

Society Finches are particularly well known for their calm and social behavior.

They are also commonly called Bengalese Finches.

One of their interesting qualities is their ability to foster and care for chicks.

They are relatively easy to breed and can coexist peacefully with compatible birds.

Society Finches occur in many color combinations, including pied, brown, white and other mutations.

Their sexes are difficult to distinguish visually because males and females have very similar appearances.

They are small birds, usually measuring approximately four to five inches.

Their average weight is around fifteen to twenty grams.

Owl Finches

Owl Finches are small Australian finches with distinctive facial markings.

They are also known as Bicheno's Finches.

Their natural habitat includes dry grasslands and other areas of northern and eastern Australia.

Their most recognizable colors include brown, white and black.

They have a pale chest crossed by dark bars and a distinctive face.

Males and females are difficult to distinguish visually.

Owl Finches are active and require sufficient room for movement and flight.

They generally do not enjoy extensive human handling.

They are small birds, generally measuring around three to four inches.

Strawberry Finch

Strawberry Finches are small birds known for their spectacular coloration.

They are also commonly known as Red Avadavats.

They originate primarily from parts of Asia and can adapt well to captivity when properly cared for.

During breeding periods, males develop an especially vivid red or scarlet appearance.

Males also have contrasting white spots and dark wings.

Females have a much more subdued coloration compared with males.

Strawberry Finches are active and social and benefit from appropriate companionship and enrichment.

They are very small birds, generally measuring approximately four inches and weighing only a few grams.
